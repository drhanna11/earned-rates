America the Beautiful Military Passes

Free annual or lifetime pass covering entrance or standard amenity (day-use) fees at lands managed by NPS, BLM, Bureau of Reclamation, USFWS, USFS, and USACE

Active dutyVeteranGuard / ReserveSpouse / familyGold Star

Who qualifies. Military Annual Pass: current members of the U.S. Armed Forces and their dependents. Military Lifetime Pass: veterans of the U.S. Armed Forces (including National Guard and Reserves) and Gold Star Families, as defined on the official page.

How to verify. In person: CAC, unexpired DD Form 1173, or Next Generation Dependent ID for the annual pass; veteran DoD ID, VHIC, VA Veteran ID Card, or state ID with veteran designation for the lifetime pass; Gold Star Families use a Gold Star Voucher. Also available via Recreation.gov or the USGS Store.

Read the terms. The pass does not cover camping, tours, special recreation permits, reservation fees, organized-group fees, or concession-operated activities. It admits the pass holder plus occupants of a single private non-commercial vehicle, or up to two motorcycles, or the pass holder and three persons 16+ where per-person fees are charged. In-person issuance is free; a digital pass is free on Recreation.gov, or a physical pass can be shipped for a $7.50 fee; USGS Store mail orders charge a $10 processing fee. Official page last updated February 26, 2026; terms recorded November 18, 2026 and re-read unchanged on November 25, 2026. This is a federal benefit, so Earned Rates does not use an affiliate link for it.
