Lowe's

Verified on November 26, 2026

Everyday Military Discount

10% off eligible full-price purchases, with no annual maximum stated on the official program page

Active dutyVeteranSpouse / family

Who qualifies. Anyone currently serving in, or a veteran of, the U.S. Army, Marine Corps, Navy, Air Force, Coast Guard, Space Force, NOAA, USPHS, or National Guard, plus the registered service member’s enrolled and verified spouse (spouse needs a separate MyLowe’s Rewards account)

How to verify. Enroll at Lowes.com/military/validation: create or sign in to a personal Lowe’s account and verify through ID.me. Online, the discount applies at checkout when you are signed in. In store, be prepared to show acceptable photo ID as listed on the official page.

Read the terms. Dishonorably discharged veterans are not eligible. The offer is non-transferable. It cannot be used on sale items or with other discounts (including Lowest Price Guarantee / Price Promise, the Lowe’s Credit 5% Off Every Day discount, volume or special pricing, and manager adjustments). It cannot be applied to major appliances (including related accessories and parts), commodities such as electrical cable and wire, dimensional lumber, plywood and OSB, gift cards, previous purchases, fees, installations, or other services (rentals, labor, delivery, shipping, extended protection plans). Digitally verified military program customers are automatically upgraded to Silver status in MyLowe’s Rewards. Terms re-checked on Lowe’s official page on November 26, 2026 (unchanged; the official FAQ still says there is no annual maximum discount, and the discount still cannot be used on any sale item).

Verified on November 18, 2026

10% off eligible in-stock purchases, with a $400 maximum annual discount that resets each calendar year

Who qualifies. Active and reservist members, veterans, and retirees of the U.S. Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Coast Guard, Space Force, NOAA, USPHS, and National Guard; the current spouse of an eligible service member; and Gold Star spouses

Exclusions include, but are not limited to, some commodity products (including lumber, wire, and building materials), value-priced merchandise, appliances, special-order items, tool rental fees, labor items, gift cards, and services including freight and delivery.
