Verified on November 23, 2026

AT&T Appreciation Savings

10% off AT&T Value 2.0, 15% off Extra 2.0, and 20% off Premium 2.0 plan lines, on up to 10 eligible phone lines. The military member must be the primary account holder.

Active dutyGuard / ReserveVeteranSpouse / family

Who qualifies. Veterans, active-duty servicemembers, reservists, and military family members (the FAQ also specifies spouses of active-duty military)

How to verify. Complete AT&T’s verification form, or sign up at an AT&T retail store. If more documentation is needed, AT&T may ask you to upload it within 14 days of wireless service activation.

Read the terms. Limited-time device and port-in promotions advertised on the same page are separate from the ongoing plan discount and have their own end dates (one bill-credit offer is listed as ending November 30, 2026). T-Mobile

Verified on November 23, 2026

T-Mix with Military Savings

Discounted T-Mix plans (Saver, Standard, More, and Loaded) for eligible military customers. T-Mobile’s official page does not publish a single percentage off standard rates.

Active dutyVeteranGuard / ReserveSpouse / familyGold Star

Who qualifies. Active-duty military, veterans, National Guard, Reserve, and Gold Star family members. The military member or Gold Star family member must be the primary account holder, and the account must maintain a valid military line.

How to verify. Visit a T-Mobile store or call (855) 623-4373 (existing customers can call 611). Verify eligible status after activation — you have 45 days, or the plan may lose Military Savings at an additional cost of up to $15/line per month.

Read the terms. No-credit-check and prepaid customers are not eligible. Government accounts are not eligible; T-Mobile for Business customers may be eligible for up to 12 lines. Common verification documents include a DD214, leave and earnings statements, and orders. For deployed active-duty members, T-Mobile says it will work with a spouse or family member to verify eligibility. T-Mobile’s FAQ says a change in military status does not affect eligibility.

Verizon

Verified on November 23, 2026

Verizon military discounts

Official military discounts on wireless plans, Fios internet, and 25% off select accessories (online only). Verizon’s military homepage we reviewed does not publish a single percentage off every wireless plan, so we do not invent one.

Active dutyRetireeVeteranGuard / ReserveGold Star

Who qualifies. U.S. active duty, retirees, veterans, current or former Guard or reserve, and Gold Star family members

How to verify. Verify military status with ID.me, then review current plan and Fios pricing on Verizon’s official military page.

Read the terms. Eligible service members generally keep the discount for life, though Verizon may occasionally ask for re-verification. Verizon will not call to ask for account information. Military business owners may receive discounts on Wireless Business Unlimited plans and Fios Business Internet. If service takes you away, Verizon lists SCRA long-term suspension plus a short-term suspend option of up to 90 days in a rolling 12-month period. Promotional prices shown on the page change; confirm the live rate.
