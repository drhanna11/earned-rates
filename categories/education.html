Southern New Hampshire University

Verified on November 18, 2026

Military online tuition rates

Online undergraduate rate of $250 per credit hour ($750 per course; $7,500 for 30 credits) and online graduate rate of $470 per credit hour ($1,410 per course; $7,050 for 15 credits) for eligible service members and spouses, versus standard online rates of $354 and $659 per credit hour. Rates are effective as of June 2026, per SNHU.

Active dutyGuard / ReserveSpouse / family

Who qualifies. U.S. service members, both full and part time (Active Duty, National Guard, Selected Reserve), and the spouses of those on active duty

How to verify. Contact SNHU Military Admission or the Military Benefits team and confirm eligibility for the military tuition rate. SNHU also works with TA, GI Bill benefits, and related programs.

Read the terms. Discounts do not apply to books or additional fees. Students receiving this rate are not eligible for additional SNHU tuition discounts. Confirm the live rate for your program. High-intent education page and a priority direct-partnership target.

University of Phoenix

Verified on November 18, 2026

Military tuition rates for service members

Undergraduate certificates, associate, and bachelor’s tuition of $750 per course ($250 per credit hour) for eligible service members. A $180 resource fee may be waived for eligible undergraduate students using Department of Defense Tuition Assistance.

Active dutyGuard / ReserveVeteranSpouse / family

Who qualifies. Military-affiliated students including service members; veterans may use GI Bill education benefits. Active and Reserve members and their families are directed to speak with their ESO or approved installation staff before enrolling.

How to verify. Start on the official University of Phoenix military page. Active service members should talk with their Education Services Officer first, then review the military tuition rates published by the university.

Read the terms. Page last updated by the university on November 2, 2026. Typical courses are 3 credits but can range from 1–6. Costs do not include transfer-credit or scholarship savings. Veterans should confirm current GI Bill use with the VA and the school. High-intent education page and a priority direct-partnership target.

Verified on November 18, 2026

Complimentary 12-month LinkedIn Premium (military community offer)

One-time complimentary 12-month LinkedIn Premium subscription. After 12 months the subscription ends automatically. Codes are first-come, first-served and the program may reach capacity.

Active dutyVeteranSpouse / family

Who qualifies. An eligible veteran of the U.S. Armed Forces, a spouse of a service member or veteran, or an active-duty service member navigating a near-term job transition, as determined by LinkedIn’s nonprofit partners.
