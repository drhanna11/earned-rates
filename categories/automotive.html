Chevrolet, Buick, GMC, and Cadillac (GM)

Verified on November 21, 2026

GM Military Appreciation Program

A special offer on eligible new Chevrolet, Buick, GMC, or Cadillac vehicles. GM’s official program page does not publish a single dollar amount. The offer can be combined with most current offers, with listed exceptions.

Active dutyGuard / ReserveVeteranRetireeSpouse / family

Who qualifies. Active Duty, Reservists, National Guard, all Veterans, and Retirees of the U.S. Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, and Coast Guard, and their sponsored spouse

How to verify. Create a GM My Account, complete registration on the Vehicle Purchase Program site, and verify through ID.me. Request an authorization number and take it to a participating U.S. GM dealer before delivery.

Read the terms. Up to two eligible new, unused vehicles per calendar year. Vehicles must be kept at least six months. Each authorization number is valid for 90 days, the dealer must have it before you take delivery, and previous purchases are not eligible. GM’s offers page says to take new retail delivery by 1/4/27. Dealer participation is voluntary. Cannot be stacked with GM First Responder, Educator, Family First, Supplier, Dealership Employee, or certain other employee/supplier programs. Program rules revised January 2, 2026. We do not invent a cash amount that GM does not publish on this page.

Ford and Lincoln

Verified on November 21, 2026

Ford and Lincoln Military Recognition Program

An exclusive cash reward toward the purchase or lease of eligible new Ford or Lincoln vehicles. The official program states the amount may vary by market; there is no single national dollar figure on the pages we used. Enter a ZIP code on fordrecognizesu.com to see the current offer. Program FAQ valid January 6, 2026–January 4, 2027.

Active dutyRetireeVeteranGuard / ReserveSpouse / family

Who qualifies. U.S. residents in the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marines, and Coast Guard (the FAQ also discusses Space Force): active duty, including National Guard, reservists serving on active duty, and DEP, plus spouse/surviving spouse and same-household members; retirees (regular, reserve, TERA, medical) plus spouse/surviving spouse and household members; honorably discharged veterans within 24 months of separation. The FAQ also says veterans may qualify for a special regional incentive regardless of separation date — check by ZIP. State Guard is not eligible.

How to verify. Verify with ID.me at fordrecognizesu.com (Find My Offer, then Confirm My ID). Bring the certificate email to the dealer.

Read the terms. Maximum five new eligible vehicle purchases or leases per calendar year. The offer can be shared with spouses, surviving spouses, and other U.S.-resident household members who register. No consumer affiliate program was found for this cash reward; it is a Verified Partner candidate. Official FAQ: https://fordrecognizesu.com/military/Home/FAQ

Hyundai

Verified on November 21, 2026

Hyundai Military Program

$500 bonus toward the purchase or lease of an eligible new Hyundai vehicle. Offer is good January 7, 2026 through December 31, 2026.

Active dutyGuard / ReserveVeteranRetireeSpouse / family

Who qualifies. Customer or spouse who is active duty, reservist/National Guard, veteran, or retired U.S. military (Air Force, Army, Coast Guard, Marine Corps, Navy, National Guard, or Reserves)

How to verify. Verify military status with ID.me before purchase, then work with a local Hyundai dealer. The bonus is applied toward MSRP and is not available as cash.

Read the terms. All new Hyundai models, including electrified vehicles, SUVs, and sedans, are listed as eligible. Not available on fleet, service rental, or demo vehicles, and not combinable with Hyundai Circle plan sales or the First Responders Program. On a lease, the rebate goes toward the amount due at signing or capitalized cost reduction; on finance, toward the down payment. Vehicle must be taken out of dealer stock.

Jeep (Stellantis)

Verified on November 21, 2026

Jeep Military Incentive

$500 Military Bonus Cash Allowance on select 2026 and 2025 Jeep vehicles

Active dutyGuard / ReserveRetireeVeteran

Who qualifies. Active, Active Reserve, Retired Military, Retired Military Reserve, 100% Disabled Veteran, or an honorably discharged veteran within 12 months of the discharge date

How to verify. Use “Get Authorization Number” (or the disabled-military button) on Jeep’s official incentive page. Present the authorization number to a franchised FCA US LLC dealer in the United States at the time of retail purchase or lease.

Read the terms. Jeep’s page lists eligible models as the 2026 Compass, Cherokee, Grand Cherokee, Wrangler ICE (excluding 392), Wrangler ICE 392, Gladiator, Grand Wagoneer, and 2025 Grand Cherokee WL 2-Row (ICE) and 3-Row and Wagoneer S. Confirm the live list before you buy — model eligibility can change. Personnel stationed overseas may have a separate path through Military AutoSource. Surviving spouses of deceased personnel who meet eligibility criteria may also receive the discount, per Jeep’s FAQ.
