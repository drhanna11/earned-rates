Nike

Verified on November 19, 2026

Nike military discount

10% off most items online and in U.S. Nike stores, applied to the first $500 of a qualified purchase

Active dutyGuard / ReserveVeteranRetireeSpouse / family

Who qualifies. Active, reservist, veteran, and retired U.S. military personnel, and the spouses and dependents of active personnel

How to verify. Online or in the app: SheerID verification, then a single-use promo code at checkout on a Nike Member profile. A new code may be requested once every seven days; the code expires two weeks after issuance. In U.S. Nike stores (not Company Stores): valid military ID at checkout.

Read the terms. Not valid on select products including Nike By You, gift cards, select Launch and SNKRS products, and Apple products; other exclusions may apply. Not redeemable on Swoosh.com. Can be combined with free-shipping promo codes only. Not valid at Nike Company Stores. The $500 cap is a frequent source of confusion — it is part of Nike’s official terms. Official terms were re-confirmed from Nike’s help-page article body on November 19, 2026 (unchanged).

lululemon

Verified on November 18, 2026

lululemon military discount

15% off purchases online and in-store on lululemon gear, including regular-price, We Made Too Much, and lululemon lab

Active dutyGuard / ReserveVeteranSpouse / family

Who qualifies. Active military members, same-household spouses and dependents (each must verify separately), reservists, and veterans, as named on the official FAQ

How to verify. SheerID via Get Verified on the official FAQ/program page. Online: sign in to the same lululemon account used to verify and turn the 15% toggle on at checkout. In-store: after online verification, bring a valid ID and ask an educator to apply it.

Read the terms. Not valid on lululemon Like New, gift cards, in combination with other promotions, or for gifting or resale. The benefit is good for four years, then re-verification is required. Cannot be applied to previous purchases. The current official rate is 15% (it was higher in earlier years). Terms recorded from the official FAQ and starter verification on November 18, 2026.
