Affiliate disclosure

Last verification pass: November 18, 2026 (Pacific Time).

Earned Rates will use affiliate links, when we are accepted into a program, so the directory can stay free to use. This page is our disclosure.

What that means

  • If you follow a marked partner link and later book or buy, we may earn a commission.
  • A commission does not change the official military rate or the eligibility rules.
  • Until an affiliate or partner ID is approved, buttons go to the official program page and are marked TODO for a later ID swap.
  • We will not replace an official military rate with a public rate just because the public rate pays a commission.
  • Some travel pages include clearly labeled sponsored travel tools, such as Discover Cars leisure rentals and Simbye travel eSIMs. These are not military discounts, and we may earn a commission if you buy through them.
  • Some official military or government rates are non-commissionable. In those cases we still send you to the official rate. Sponsored Verified Partner listings are how we can fund those pages honestly.

How we choose links

We favor programs that pay on travel bookings, high-ticket electronics, and education, and that can sit beside an official military discount without undermining it. We deprioritize low-margin, short-cookie marketplace links as a primary revenue plan. Details of the programs we are applying to are kept in our internal partner files; this page will be updated when live affiliate IDs are in use.

This disclosure is dated November 18, 2026. It is written to be consistent with ordinary U.S. advertising and FTC endorsement guidance: if a link can pay us, we say so.

Questions about this disclosure or a Verified Partner listing: contact@earnedrates.com.
