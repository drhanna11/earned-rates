About Earned Rates and how we verify

Last verification pass: November 18, 2026 (Pacific Time).

Earned Rates exists so service members and their families do not have to hunt through expired listicles to find a benefit they already earned. We are building a careful, current directory — patriotic in substance, not in decoration.

How we verify

A program is listed only when we can point to an official brand or school page and record what that page actually says. We do not invent rates, coupon codes, or eligibility. If a brand does not publish a percentage or a dollar amount, we say so and send you to the official page.

  1. We open the official program page (the brand’s own site, not a third-party recap).
  2. We record the benefit, who qualifies, how to verify, important exclusions, and the official URL.
  3. We stamp the card with the date of that check — currently November 18, 2026.
  4. If a direct fetch is blocked, and we still have the official page’s published text from that day, we say so on the card and ask you to re-check the official page.
  5. If we cannot confirm a live program — as with Hyatt’s older MILVET announcement, which we could not re-confirm on a live Hyatt page on this date — we leave it out until we can.
We will not list a “military discount” that exists only on a blog, a Facebook comment, or an expired code site. Those are the listings that waste a service member’s time.

Verified Partner listings

Featured “Verified Partner” slots on this site are reserved for brands that already offer a confirmed military discount and want a sponsored placement. The rules do not relax for a paying partner: official terms, clear eligibility, and a verification date still have to be on the page. Partner revenue helps us keep the directory current. It will never be a reason to invent a better-sounding rate.

Some official military or government rates are non-commissionable. In those cases a sponsored placement, or a direct brand partnership, is the honest way to fund the page — not a quiet redirect to a weaker public rate.

Who we are for

Active-duty members, veterans, retirees, National Guard and Reserve, and the spouses, families, and Gold Star families that official programs include. Eligibility differs by brand. Read the card, then confirm on the official page.

What we will not do

  • We will not dress this up as a giveaway or a “hack.”
  • We will not bury official rates under a higher-commission booking engine.
  • We will not claim a Department of Defense or VA endorsement we do not have.
