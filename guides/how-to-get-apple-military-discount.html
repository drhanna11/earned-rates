How to get the Apple military discount

Last verification pass: November 18, 2026 (Pacific Time).

Apple’s official Veterans and Military Purchase Program offers a 10% discount on Apple products and accessories to current and Veteran members of the U.S. Military, National Guard, and Reserve, and to immediate family members who live in the same household. The steps below follow Apple’s official store page, last checked November 18, 2026.

Steps

  1. Open Apple’s official Veterans and Military Store.
  2. Verify your veteran or active military status with ID.me, as Apple instructs on that page.
  3. Shop the official store. The 10% program discount is the benefit Apple publishes for this store.
  4. Use a payment method the offer allows. Apple states that Apple Card Monthly Installments and Apple Upgrade are not available for this offer.

The program is for personal use, not resale. Apple may refuse or cancel an order it suspects is for resale. Family eligibility is limited to immediate family in the same household. If ID.me cannot verify you automatically, follow ID.me’s document prompts — do not use an unofficial “Apple military code” from a third-party site.
