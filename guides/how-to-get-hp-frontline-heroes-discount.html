How to get the HP Frontline Heroes discount

Last verification pass: November 26, 2026 (Pacific Time).

Verified Nov 26, 2026

HP’s official Frontline Heroes Store offers “up to 40% in exclusive savings” on HP.com for the military community, alongside healthcare workers and first responders. It is an official HP program — but the discount only appears after you verify with ID.me and finish HP’s own store registration. This guide follows HP’s official page and program terms as we read them on November 18, 2026.

Open the official HP Frontline Heroes Store

Who qualifies

HP’s terms say the program is open to “military and veterans and their families” (as well as first responders, nurses, doctors, medical providers, and hospital staff) who reside in the United States. HP requires a valid ID.me account with one of those designated group affiliations to register. HP does not publish a list of accepted documents; ID.me handles that step.

Steps

  1. Open HP’s official Frontline Heroes Store page on hp.com. Start there, not from a coupon site.
  2. Select Verify with ID.me and sign in to (or create) your ID.me account. Confirm your military, veteran, or military-family affiliation.
  3. When ID.me sends you back to HP, complete the HP Store registration. This is the step people miss: an ID.me verification alone does not show you member pricing.
  4. Shop while signed in. Returning members can skip ID.me and sign in under Sign in/Register in the upper right of the Frontline Heroes page.
  5. Compare the member price with HP’s current public sale price before you check out. HP’s coupons page says you can typically use one discount type, not a combination, on an order.

What the program covers — and what it does not

  • Qualifying categories in HP’s terms: select consumer PCs (HP, Pavilion, Envy, Spectre, and Omen), select business PCs (Elite, Pro, and Workstation), select consumer, business, and third-party accessories, and select printers.
  • Yearly limits: 2 PCs, 2 tablets, 10 accessories (including Care Packs), 2 printers, and 12 supplies per calendar year. HP says exceeding these limits can result in removal from the program.
  • Ink and toner have not been eligible for program discounts since March 1, 2023.
  • HP.com only. Participation is valid only at hp.com — not at other retailers that sell HP products.
  • Personal use. Purchases are for end users, not businesses or resellers, and are separate from the HP Loyalty / Referral program.

HP states it may change or end the program at any time. The yearly limits and exclusions above come from HP’s program terms on hp.com; the “up to 40%” figure is HP’s published ceiling, not a guaranteed percentage on every product. Check the official page before a large purchase.

Common questions

I verified with ID.me, but I still see regular prices. What happened?

Usually the HP Store registration was not finished, or you are not signed in. Return to the official Frontline Heroes page, sign in under Sign in/Register, and look for member pricing. If the ID.me pop-up never appears, a blocked pop-up window or a browser extension may be the cause; try another browser or device. HP’s coupons and promotions page also invites U.S. military customers to contact HP to get signed up, and notes that login is required to see your discounts.

HP says my email is already linked to another account.

That is an HP account issue, not an eligibility decision. Contact HP Store customer service from the official site rather than creating workarounds, and keep your ID.me verification as it is.

Is there an HP military promo code?

HP’s official program does not use a public military code — pricing appears in the signed-in Frontline Heroes Store. Codes posted on third-party coupon sites are not the official program, and seasonal offers (for example, Veterans Day promotions at various brands) expire. If a listing does not lead to HP’s own page, treat it as unverified.

Can my spouse or family use it?

HP’s terms name military and veterans “and their families” residing in the U.S. Family members still need an ID.me account with an eligible designated affiliation.

Does it work in an HP-branded store at a mall or big-box retailer?

No. HP says participation is valid only at hp.com.
