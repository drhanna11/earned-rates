How to book official military hotel rates

Last verification pass: November 20, 2026 (Pacific Time).

Military hotel rates are earned benefits, not secret coupon codes. Each chain has its own booking path. Use the official program page, select the military or government rate in the brand’s own search, and bring ID to the desk.

General steps

  1. Decide whether you are traveling on official orders or on leisure. Some rates are leisure only (IHG’s Military Appreciation Rate is one example). Official travel may use a government per diem rate instead.
  2. Open the official chain page linked from our hotel comparison.
  3. Search dates and the city or base. Hilton’s official page notes that you can search by military base.
  4. Select the military, veteran, or government special rate — not a third-party “deal.”
  5. Enter an official rate code only when the brand publishes one. Wyndham publishes SML for its Military Appreciation Rate. We do not publish unofficial codes that circulate on blogs.
  6. Join the loyalty program if the brand requires it for the leisure rate (Choice Privileges is required for Choice’s leisure military rate).
  7. Bring valid military, veteran, or government ID to check-in. If the desk cannot see the rate, ask them to look up the official military or government rate for those dates.

Chain notes, as of November 20, 2026

  • IHG. Leisure only; minimum 5% off Best Flexible Rate at participating hotels; not at IHG Army Hotels; ID at check-in.
  • Hilton. Per diem and a Military Family Rate for leisure; ID at check-in; no chain-wide percentage published.
  • Wyndham. At least 15% off Standard Rate at participating U.S. and Canadian hotels; code SML; ID at check-in.
  • Choice. Leisure rate requires Choice Privileges membership and an eligible guest in the room.
  • Best Western. At or below per diem in North America; official ID or work orders at check-in.

We did not list a chain-wide Marriott leisure program because we could not find a current family-wide official leisure page with clear published terms. Individual Marriott hotels may offer government or appreciation rates; confirm at the property. Hyatt’s 2018 MILVET announcement was not re-confirmed on a live Hyatt program page (re-checked November 22, 2026; Hyatt’s page still returned an error to our checker), so it is not listed.

Booking near a specific installation? See Fort Bragg and Fayetteville (IHG Army Hotels on post — where IHG’s leisure military rate does not apply), Naval Base San Diego (including Navy Lodge and the PCS lodging rule), or Naval Station Norfolk.

Traveling overseas on leave or a PCS move? Sponsored travel tool: Simbye travel eSIM data plans. Simbye code EARNED15 takes 15% off. This is not a military discount, and we may earn a commission. If you are deployed or on duty, follow your unit’s rules on personal phones and devices before you use any eSIM.
