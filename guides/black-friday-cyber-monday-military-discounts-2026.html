Black Friday & Cyber Monday 2026: which military discounts stack with sale prices

Last verification pass: November 26, 2026 (Pacific Time).

Verified Nov 26, 2026 Dated page — ends Dec 3, 2026

Black Friday is Friday, November 27, 2026, and Cyber Week runs through Thursday, December 3. The question military families keep asking this week is simple: does my military discount come off on top of the sale price? Usually it does not — but a few programs do stack, one gives you the better of the two automatically, and several cap the discount in ways that matter on a big holiday order. We read each brand’s own terms on Thanksgiving night, November 26, 2026. Nothing here is a code we made up or a percentage copied from a list.

See what stacks Exchange Black Friday deals

The short answer

  • Stacks (per the brand’s own page): Samsung Offer Program — Samsung’s FAQ says the military savings “can be combined with public and seasonal offers.” At the Exchange, a new MILITARY STAR cardmember’s 10% first-day discount combines with Exchange promotions.
  • Better of the two, automatically: Microsoft Store — sign in and the cart shows whichever is deeper, the military discount or the sale price.
  • Pick one: Lowe’s, Under Armour, lululemon, Foot Locker, and Nike (Nike’s code combines only with free-shipping codes). On a doorbuster, the sale price may beat the military discount — do the math.
  • Partly or not stated: Dell combines with “select offers” but not other Dell coupons, and excludes limited-quantity deals. HP, GE Appliances, adidas, and Carhartt do not say on the pages we read — compare the two prices in your cart.

Four ways a holiday order goes wrong

  1. The cap, not the percentage, sets your savings. Nike’s 10% applies to the first $500 of a purchase, so a $900 order saves at most $50. Foot Locker caps each verification at $100 off.
  2. You spend a yearly allowance on a small item. Dell allows two discounted transactions a year (two products each). HP’s program allows 2 PCs and 2 printers per calendar year, and exceeding the limits can remove you from the program. Samsung allows two products per category in any 12 months. Save these for the big purchase.
  3. Appliances are excluded where you expect them. Lowe’s excludes major appliances, and our Home Depot card lists appliances as excluded too. Military pricing on appliances, where it exists, is in brand programs such as the GE Appliances private store and the Samsung Offer Program.
  4. The “military” deal is on a look-alike site. Only verify through the brand’s own domain (or the ID.me / SheerID flow it launches). Scam “military discount” pages and repost codes circulate every holiday.
