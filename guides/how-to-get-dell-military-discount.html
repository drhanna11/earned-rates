How to get the Dell military discount

Last verification pass: November 18, 2026 (Pacific Time).

Verified Nov 19, 2026

Dell’s official Heroes Purchase Program gives verified active-duty service members, veterans, and retirees a member discount on eligible PCs and electronics at Dell.com. There is no code to hunt for: you verify once with SheerID inside your Dell account, and the discount is applied in your cart. The catch is the fine print — two eligible products per order, two orders a year — so plan the big purchase. This guide follows Dell’s official Heroes page as we read it on November 19, 2026.

Open Dell’s official Heroes page

Who qualifies

Dell’s official military FAQ names three groups who complete verification through SheerID:

  • Active-duty military — current members of the U.S. Armed Forces.
  • Veterans — former members of the U.S. Armed Forces.
  • Retirees — retired members of the U.S. Armed Forces.

Dell’s page does not name spouses, dependents, or Gold Star families for the military discount. If you see a list that says otherwise, it is not quoting Dell’s current page.

Steps

  1. Sign in or create a Dell account on Dell.com. If you already have one, Dell says you do not need a new one.
  2. Register for Dell Rewards. Dell lists this as part of the sign-up; it is free for U.S. customers.
  3. Verify with SheerID. In your account’s membership section, select the military discount program and click Verify. Enter your details exactly as they appear on your official documents, and upload a document if SheerID asks for one.
  4. Shop while signed in. Dell says the discount is applied automatically to eligible products in your cart at checkout — it may not show on every product listing page.
  5. Check the cart before you pay. Compare the cart price with Dell’s current public promotions; the military discount cannot be stacked with other Dell coupons.

Dell says verification is usually almost instant. When SheerID needs to review documents by hand, Dell says it typically takes about 20 minutes in the United States.

Limits and exclusions

  • Two eligible products per transaction, and two transactions per year per eligible customer. If you are outfitting a family or a home office, decide which items matter most.
  • Dell.com only. Other retailers that sell Dell products do not accept it.
  • Coupons: combinable with select offers, but not stackable with other Dell coupons.
  • Excluded: limited-quantity deals, certain order codes, clearance offers, warranties, gift cards, and some PCs, electronics, and accessories.

Common questions

I verified, but I do not see a discount.

Dell’s page lists three checks: make sure you are signed in to the verified Dell account, confirm SheerID shows your status as verified, and make sure the items in your cart are eligible. The discount is applied in the cart, not always on the product page.

My SheerID verification is pending or failed. Who do I contact?

Dell says verification problems go to SheerID (through the SheerID Help Center). If SheerID has confirmed you but the discount still will not apply on Dell.com, contact Dell sales through Dell’s Contact Us page.

Is there a Dell military promo code?

Not on Dell’s official page. The discount is tied to your verified Dell account and applied automatically. Codes on coupon sites are not the official military program.

Do I earn Dell Rewards on the purchase?

Dell Rewards is a separate free loyalty program; Dell says members earn points on eligible Dell.com purchases. Check your cart and Dell Rewards terms for what applies to a given order.
