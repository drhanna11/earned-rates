Official military rates near Naval Station Norfolk

Last verification pass: November 18, 2026 (Pacific Time).

Naval Station Norfolk sits in Norfolk, Virginia, in the Hampton Roads area, near Norfolk International Airport (ORF) and a short drive from other Southside and Peninsula communities. If you are arriving on orders, taking leave, or visiting family, you can use official chain military rates in this area the same way you would in any other U.S. city — by booking the official program and bringing ID.

Book official chain rates, not a guessed local price

We have not independently verified a specific Norfolk hotel’s private military rate for this page. Use the official programs below and search for Norfolk, VA, or, where the brand allows it, for Naval Station Norfolk itself.

  • Hilton. Hilton’s official government and military page states you can search by military base. Start there, then present ID at check-in. Official Hilton page.
  • IHG. Book the leisure Military Appreciation Rate at participating hotels (minimum 5% off Best Flexible Rate overall; not at IHG Army Hotels). Official IHG page.
  • Wyndham. Participating U.S. hotels, rate code SML, at least 15% off Standard Rate, ID at check-in. Official Wyndham page.
  • Choice Hotels. Leisure military/veteran rate at participating hotels; Choice Privileges membership required. Official Choice page.
  • Best Western. Government and military discounts at or below per diem in North America, with official ID at check-in. Official Best Western page.

Cars at ORF and in Norfolk

For leisure rentals, start with official military rates: Enterprise’s 5% leisure discount, Hertz’s official leisure government booking path, and Avis’s official military offers. Official travel on orders may use contracted government rates instead. See the car rental comparison.

Orders versus leave

If you are on official travel, ask whether a government per diem rate, rather than a leisure military rate, is the correct booking. Keep orders or a government travel card available if the official program requires them. Leisure rates, including IHG’s Military Appreciation Rate, are not a substitute for official-travel policy.
