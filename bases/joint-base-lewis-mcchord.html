Military hotel and car rental rates near Joint Base Lewis-McChord (JBLM) and Tacoma, WA

Last verification pass: November 24, 2026 (Pacific Time).

Verified on November 24, 2026

Joint Base Lewis-McChord, home of America’s I Corps and the 62nd Airlift Wing, sits along Interstate 5 in Pierce County, south of Tacoma and next to Lakewood. Military OneSource puts it about 45 miles south of Seattle. Soldiers and Airmen, and the families who follow them, arrive through Sea-Tac and split between two very different front gates: Lewis Main (I-5 Exit 120) and McChord Field (I-5 Exit 125). This page puts on-post lodging, the official chain military rates, and the Sea-Tac rental programs in one place, each checked against its own official page on November 24, 2026.

Quick answer. On orders, start with IHG Army Hotels on post: Rainier Inn on Lewis Main or Candlewood Suites on McChord Field. Pick the side where you report: Lewis Main is I-5 Exit 120 and McChord Field is Exit 125. On leave or a family visit off post, compare Wyndham (at least 15% off with code SML), IHG’s Military Appreciation Rate (minimum 5%, leisure only, off-post hotels only), and Hilton’s Military Family Rate, then bring ID to the desk. For a car at Sea-Tac, start with Enterprise’s 5% leisure discount or Avis’s veteran and military family AWD.

Orders or leave? Start here

  • PCS orders. On-post lodging at JBLM is run by IHG Army Hotels under the Privatization of Army Lodging (PAL) program. IHG Army Hotels’ FAQ says official travelers at these installations no longer need a Statement of Non-Availability for lodging reimbursement, and they will not issue one. Ask your finance office how Temporary Lodging Expense applies to your move; this page does not decide reimbursement. Military OneSource says Army personnel report to the Installation Reception Center (Building 2021, Pendleton Avenue) and Air Force personnel report to their unit Commander’s Support Staff.
  • Official travel (TDY). Book through your travel system or Travel Management Company. The FY2027 GSA per diem for Tacoma (Pierce County), which covers ZIP 98433 on Lewis Main, is $143 per night for lodging, the same every month, and $86 for meals and incidentals, per the GSA per diem lookup. Your orders and your finance office decide what is reimbursable.
  • Leave or a family visit. Use a leisure military rate. Leisure rates such as IHG’s Military Appreciation Rate are not a substitute for official-travel policy.
  • Guests without a DoD ID. A hotel reservation does not get you through the gate. Military OneSource says vehicles without an adult, authorized DoD ID cardholder must stop at the Visitor Control Center next to the Lewis Main Liberty Gate (I-5 Exit 120) or the McChord Field Main Gate (I-5 Exit 125) for a temporary pass. Bring a valid driver’s license, vehicle registration, and proof of insurance, and have an adult, authorized sponsor available by phone. Gate hours change; check JBLM’s visitor and gate page before you drive.

Rental cars at Seattle-Tacoma International Airport (SEA)

Military OneSource says most people flying to JBLM land at Sea-Tac. The Port of Seattle says every rental company at SEA works out of one off-site Rental Car Facility (3150 S. 160th St., SeaTac). Free shuttles run 24 hours a day from the north and south ends of the baggage-claim level, and the ride takes about five minutes. The Port lists Alamo, Avis, Budget, Dollar, Enterprise, Fox, Hertz, National, Payless, Sixt, and Thrifty. From the airport, Military OneSource’s directions are I-5 South to Exit 125 for McChord Field or Exit 120 for Lewis Main. If you are on PCS orders and take a rideshare or taxi instead, Military OneSource says to keep the receipt for your travel voucher. The Sea-Tac USO Center, outside security on the mezzanine level above Southwest’s ticket counter, is free for service members and families; hours vary.

On official travel? Use the U.S. Government Rental Car Program instead of a leisure discount. DTMO says to book the government rate through your agency travel system or Travel Management Company, present orders, a government ID, or a Government Travel Charge Card, and make sure your department or agency name is on the rental contract. Program benefits include LDW/CDW and liability coverage at no extra cost, no blackout dates, and no additional-driver fee for authorized drivers 21 and older. Hertz’s official page notes a $5-per-day Government Administrative Rate Supplement on official travel, as the government agreement requires.

Just arrived? Ask about Tuition Assistance

A November 20, 2026 GAO report (GAO-27-107854) found that the Air Force and Navy do not require their bases to brief Tuition Assistance at orientation for newly assigned members, even though DOD policy says the services will provide it. That matters at a joint base: if you are reporting to McChord Field, or if nobody mentions TA after you sign in on Lewis Main, visit the education center and ask.
