City and base page template

Last verification pass: November 18, 2026 (Pacific Time).

This is the template for a city or base page. Each nightly or weekly page should help someone who is moving, visiting, or taking leave near a specific installation — without inventing a hotel’s private rate.

What belongs on a base page

  • The official name of the installation and the nearby city or cities.
  • How to use official chain military rates (IHG, Hilton, Wyndham, Choice, Best Western) when you search that city or, where the brand allows it, the base itself.
  • A reminder to distinguish official travel (orders, per diem) from leisure leave.
  • Links to official rental-car military rates for airport or city locations serving that area.
  • Only a property-specific rate that we have verified on that hotel’s official page. If we have not, we do not invent one.

What does not belong

Unsourced “nearby hotels from $89” modules, unofficial rate codes, or a map of prices we did not check. A useful base page is a guide to official programs in a real place — not a fake booking engine.
