Military hotel and car rental rates near Naval Base San Diego

Last verification pass: November 20, 2026 (Pacific Time).

Naval Base San Diego sits on San Diego Bay, about six miles from San Diego International Airport by the Navy Lodge’s own directions, and the San Diego area is a very large duty station. Military OneSource warns that Navy Lodge demand here “far exceeds availability.” This page puts the on-base options, the official chain military rates, and the airport rental programs in one place, each checked against its own official page on November 20, 2026.

Quick answer. On PCS orders, check the Navy Lodge first — it is required before you can be reimbursed for commercial lodging under the current Temporary Lodging Expense policy. On leave, compare Wyndham (at least 15% off with code SML), IHG (minimum 5%, leisure only), and Hilton’s Military Family Rate, then bring ID to the desk. For a car at SAN, start with Enterprise’s 5% leisure discount or Avis’s veteran and military family AWD.

Orders or leave? Start here

  • PCS orders. Military OneSource’s Naval Base San Diego page says the current Temporary Lodging Expense policy requires PCS travelers to check Navy Lodge availability at area installations first. If nothing is available, the Navy Lodge issues a certificate of non-availability so you can be reimbursed for non-government lodging. Mention your PCS orders when you call — you may receive priority.
  • Official travel (TDY/TAD). Book through your travel system or Travel Management Company and use the government rate. The FY2027 GSA per diem for San Diego County is $208 per night for lodging ($249 in June and July) and $86 for meals and incidentals, per the GSA per diem lookup. Your orders and your finance office decide what is reimbursable — not this page.
  • Leave or a family visit. Use a leisure military rate. Leisure rates such as IHG’s Military Appreciation Rate are not a substitute for official-travel policy.

Rental cars at San Diego International (SAN)

Every rental pick-up and drop-off at SAN happens at the airport’s Rental Car Center (3355 Admiral Boland Way), with free shuttles running 24/7 from the terminals. Enterprise, Avis, and Hertz all operate there.

On official travel? Use the U.S. Government Rental Car Program instead of a leisure discount. DTMO says to book through your agency travel system or Travel Management Company, present orders, a government ID, or a Government Travel Charge Card, and confirm your department or agency name is on the rental contract. Program benefits include LDW/CDW and liability coverage at no extra cost and no additional-driver fee for authorized drivers 21 and older.
