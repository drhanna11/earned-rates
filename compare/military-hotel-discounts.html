Wyndham Hotels & Resorts

Verified on November 24, 2026

Military Appreciation Rate

At least 15% off the property’s Standard Rate at participating U.S. and Canadian hotels

Active dutyVeteranRetireeGuard / ReserveSpouse / family

Who qualifies. U.S. or Canadian active, reserve, veteran, or retired members, and their spouses

How to verify. Book with rate code SML. Show valid military identification at check-in. Wyndham Rewards membership is not required for the rate. Confirming military status can also upgrade eligible members to Gold and add 3,000 bonus points after the first qualifying night.

Read the terms. Participating brands are listed in Wyndham’s terms. New bookings only; not for groups. A credit card is required to hold the reservation, and the official terms say the rate is cancellable by 4 p.m. local time on the arrival date. Cannot be combined with other discounts. Blackout dates and room exclusions may apply. Rate is based on double occupancy.
