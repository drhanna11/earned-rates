United Airlines

Verified on November 18, 2026

Active-Duty Military Leisure Discount and travel benefits

5% off the published base fare on select United and United Express operated flights purchased on united.com or the United app, plus checked-bag and boarding benefits described on the official page

Active dutyGuard / Reserve

Who qualifies. Active-duty U.S. Army, Navy, Air Force, Marines, Space Force, and Coast Guard for the 5% leisure discount. Broader bag and boarding benefits also cover academy cadets/midshipmen, USPHS and NOAA commissioned officers, Reserve and National Guard on active orders, and members discharged within 7 days of scheduled travel.

How to verify. Verify active-duty status in your MileagePlus profile, then book on united.com or the app. Eligible flights are marked with a discount applied. The active-duty MileagePlus account holder must be on the itinerary.

Read the terms. Flights must originate in the U.S., Guam, Puerto Rico, the U.S. Virgin Islands, or Canada, and be fully operated by United or United Express. Up to nine passengers per itinerary. Basic Economy is not eligible. The discount cannot be combined with other travel discount certificates, discounted fares, or United Vacations discounts. It does not cover government-imposed taxes, fees, or carrier-imposed international surcharges. United Vacations has a separate military travel discount for flight + hotel packages.

American Airlines

Verified on November 18, 2026

Military fares, bags, and boarding

Government or military fares in some markets (call Reservations). Free checked bags for active U.S. military with ID: up to 3 bags, 50 lbs each, on personal/leisure travel; up to 5 bags, 100 lbs each, for active military and/or dependents traveling on orders.

Active dutySpouse / family

Who qualifies. Active U.S. military with ID; dependents traveling on orders for the five-bag allowance

How to verify. Call American Reservations for military or government fares. Present a valid active U.S. military ID for bag and boarding benefits. Benefits apply on American marketed and operated itineraries, not partner-operated codeshares.

Read the terms. Active-duty military with ID may board in Group 1. American also mentions vacation savings through a WeSalute+ subscription; that path is a third-party membership, not an American published fare percentage.

Delta Air Lines

Verified on November 18, 2026

Military baggage allowance and related benefits

Free checked bags for active U.S. military: 5 bags on military orders; for personal travel, 2 bags in Delta Main/Comfort (including Basic experiences) and 3 bags in Premium Select, Delta First, and Delta One. Delta’s official page also states there is a military discount on flights and Delta Vacations, but it does not publish a percentage on that baggage page.

Active duty

Who qualifies. Active members of the U.S. Marine Corps, Army, Air Force, Space Force, Navy, or Coast Guard

How to verify. Review Delta’s official military baggage page and present required military identification at the airport. Confirm any flight discount at booking; we do not list an unpublished percentage.

Read the terms. On orders, bags may be up to 80 linear inches and under 100 lbs as shown in Delta’s baggage key. For leisure, bags may be up to 62 linear inches. Benefits are subject to embargoes and regional weight restrictions and apply to Delta-marketed tickets, including certain partner-operated flights listed on the page (Aeromexico, Air France, KLM, Korean Air, and Virgin Atlantic).

Southwest Airlines

Verified on November 18, 2026

Military fares, bags, and priority boarding

Military fares are offered but are not available on Southwest.com — book by phone or at a ticket counter. Active-duty customers are exempt from the two-piece bag limit and from excess, oversize, and overweight bag charges if no bag exceeds 100 lbs or 80 inches.

Active dutyGuard / ReserveSpouse / family

Who qualifies. U.S. military personnel on active duty (including reservists, National Guard, and Coast Guard members with active orders, plus cadets/midshipmen at the U.S. service academies) and their authorized dependents for military fares. Bag and boarding benefits described below are for active-duty members with valid ID.

How to verify. Call 1-800-I-FLY-SWA (1-800-435-9792) or visit a Southwest ticket counter to book a military fare. Present a current, valid Uniformed Services ID at check-in for bag and boarding benefits.

Read the terms. Active-duty members with valid Uniformed Services ID are invited to board in the Priority Boarding group, just before Group 1. Dependents traveling without the active-duty member are subject to normal bag charges. No more than 20 checked pieces per ticketed customer. Rapid Rewards points cannot be redeemed to purchase government and military fares. Southwest also refunds personal travel that conflicts with military or government orders, with supporting documentation.
