HP

Verified on November 22, 2026

HP Frontline Heroes Store

Exclusive savings of up to 40% for eligible members of the military community and other frontline groups listed in HP’s terms

Active dutyVeteranSpouse / family

Who qualifies. Military and veterans and their families who reside in the United States (the store also serves healthcare workers and first responders)

How to verify. Select Verify with ID.me on the official HP Frontline Heroes page, confirm a designated military group affiliation, and complete the HP Store registration. Returning members can skip ID.me and sign in under Sign in/Register.

Read the terms. Valid only on HP.com. HP’s program terms limit purchases to 2 PCs, 2 tablets, 10 accessories (including Care Packs), 2 printers, and 12 supplies per calendar year; exceeding them can remove you from the program. Ink and toner have not been eligible for program discounts since March 1, 2023. Not combinable with the HP Loyalty / Referral program; end-user purchases only, not for resale. The “up to 40%” figure is HP’s published ceiling; actual savings vary by product.

Dell

Verified on November 24, 2026

Dell Heroes / Military Discount

Exclusive member discounts on eligible PCs and electronics on Dell.com. Dell’s official Heroes page does not publish a single percentage.

Active dutyVeteranRetiree

Who qualifies. Active-duty U.S. military members, veterans, and retirees who complete SheerID verification

How to verify. Create or sign in to a Dell account, register for Dell Rewards, and verify military status with SheerID from the account membership section. The discount is then applied automatically to eligible products at checkout on Dell.com.

Read the terms. Valid only on Dell.com, not at other retailers. Up to two eligible products per transaction, and up to two transactions per year per eligible customer. Can be combined with select offers but not stacked with other Dell coupons. Some PCs, electronics, accessories, warranties, gift cards, clearance, and limited-quantity deals are excluded. Dell says verification is usually almost instant; a document review, when required, typically takes about 20 minutes in the U.S. Dell’s official page names active-duty members, veterans, and retirees; it does not publish a percentage or name spouses or families. Re-checked November 24, 2026 (unchanged).

Samsung

Verified on November 22, 2026

Samsung Offer Program — Military

Exclusive savings up to 30% off, which Samsung says can be combined with public and seasonal offers. The amount of savings differs across product lines.

Active dutyVeteranRetiree

Who qualifies. Active military members (including those with a .mil email), veterans, and retired personnel

How to verify. Create a Samsung Account, then verify through ID.me (or, as Samsung also notes, through a benefits provider such as GovX or WeSalute).

Read the terms. Purchase limits: up to two products per product category, per email and/or shipping address, in any 12-month period, with higher limits on some accessory categories. Samsung.com sign-in is required.

Microsoft Store

Verified on November 24, 2026

Military and veteran discount

Up to 10% off select physical consumer products from the Microsoft Store

Active dutyVeteranRetireeSpouse / family

Who qualifies. Active, former, and retired military members, and their immediate families
