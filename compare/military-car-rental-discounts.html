Enterprise Rent-A-Car

Verified on November 18, 2026

Military & Government Leisure Discount

5% off base rates for Government Leisure Travel. Official Government Travel uses contracted rates, not this leisure percentage.

Active dutyVeteranRetiree

Who qualifies. Current U.S. federal employees, active-duty members, retired employees, and veterans

How to verify. Book through Enterprise’s official military and government discounts page. No military-association membership is required. Military or government identification may be requested at pick-up.

Read the terms. Leisure renters must be 21 or older. Official travel may be available from age 18 (a young-driver surcharge may apply). The leisure discount is accepted at Enterprise locations worldwide, according to the official FAQ. USAA members have a separate member offer.

Avis

Verified on November 18, 2026

Military discounts via WeSalute, plus a veteran/military family AWD

Avis states that active military, veterans, and their families can receive military discounts when they enroll in WeSalute. Avis’s official coupons page also lists AWD T765700 as up to 25% off base rates for veteran and military family.

Active dutyVeteranSpouse / family

Who qualifies. Active military, veterans, and their families, as stated on Avis’s official coupons and offers page

How to verify. Review the current AWD and WeSalute instructions on Avis’s official offers page. Enter the AWD at the start of booking. Bring ID if the reservation requires it.

Read the terms. WeSalute publishes its own “up to 35% off Pay Now” figure on WeSalute’s site. That figure is WeSalute’s claim, not a percentage we independently confirmed on an Avis program homepage. Use Avis’s own current AWD and terms when you book. AWDs must be entered at the beginning of the reservation.
